# Evidence and Copyright

Distill only content-independent traits:

- tone traits and avoided tendencies;
- narrative pace and sentence variation;
- paragraph-length range;
- argument moves and opening/ending patterns;
- component appearance, spacing, hierarchy, placement, and frequency;
- article-type recipes.

Do not retain or reproduce article paragraphs, signature phrases, slogans, examples, claims, brand marks, or author identity as generative instructions. Evidence artifacts may keep source identifiers, statistical summaries, rule meanings, sample IDs, and confidence.

Describe the result as an original reusable profile informed by references, not as a clone of a living author. Where evidence conflicts, preserve the disagreement and lower confidence. Where layout evidence is absent, explicitly return writing-only.
